How EduShare Operates

A strict, trusted academic workflow designed for quality control and institutional security.

1️⃣

Student Registration

Students register with USN, branch, semester & section. Registrations default to PENDING status and cannot access the student dashboard until verified.

2️⃣

Admin Verification

Campus administrator reviews student credentials. Once approved, the student gains access to browse and download approved course material.

3️⃣

Upload Permission

Approval alone does NOT grant upload capability. Administrators manually grant explicit upload authorization to select, trusted students.

4️⃣

Note Quality Review

Uploaded notes enter a pending moderation queue. Administrators review the file before approving, ensuring only authentic materials reach students.
